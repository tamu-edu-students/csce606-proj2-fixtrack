# FixTrack

FixTrack is a cloud-hosted equipment maintenance and repair tracker for small
engineering teams, laboratories, workshops, and maintenance organizations. It
centralizes equipment records, maintenance tickets, technician assignments,
ticket status, and repair history in one application.

## Product Goal

FixTrack helps a maintenance team track equipment problems from initial report
through resolution. Managers can manage equipment and assign work, while
technicians can update assigned tickets and document completed repairs.

The MVP includes:

- User authentication with manager and technician roles
- Equipment management
- Maintenance ticket creation and tracking
- Technician assignment and ticket workflow
- Repair records and equipment history
